Privacy Policy

Countdown · Last updated October 28, 2026

English简体中文

The short version: Countdown doesn't collect any personal data. Everything you add stays on your iPhone. We don't have servers, accounts, ads or analytics.

What you add stays on your device

The names, dates, notes and photos you add are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared by the app and its widgets. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

When you add a background or memory photo, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. The app only receives the photos you pick and keeps its own copy on your device. It can't see the rest of your photo library.

If you save a share image, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photo library. This permission only lets the app add images, not read them.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent over the internet to deliver them.

Widgets and Live Activities

Widgets, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities show the day you choose. Keep in mind that anyone who can see your Home Screen or Lock Screen can see them too.

Backups

When you back up, the app creates a file with your days, notes and photos and saves it where you choose, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. That file is yours. Storing it is handled by you and the service you choose, under their own terms. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

Countdown Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles payment. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether you have an active purchase so it can unlock Pro features. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

No tracking

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No third-party code that collects data

Children

Countdown doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any day in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected, so delete those yourself if you no longer want them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email luckytonyma@gmail.com.

隐私政策

倒数日 · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 28 日

简单来说：倒数日不收集任何个人数据。你添加的所有内容都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。我们没有服务器，没有账号，没有广告，也没有统计分析。

你添加的内容只保存在设备上

你添加的名称、日期、文字和照片只保存在你的 iPhone 上，由 APP 和它的小组件共用。这些内容不会发送给我们，也不会发送给任何其他人。

照片

添加背景照片或回忆照片时，你通过苹果的照片选择器来选。APP 只会拿到你选中的照片，并在设备上保存一份副本，看不到你相册里的其他照片。

保存分享图片时，iOS 会请求「添加到相册」的权限。这个权限只能往相册里添加图片，不能读取相册。

提醒

提醒是在你的 iPhone 上安排的本地通知，送达时不会通过网络传输任何内容。

小组件和实时活动

桌面小组件、锁屏小组件和实时活动会显示你选择的纪念日。请注意，能看到你主屏幕或锁屏的人也能看到这些内容。

备份

备份时，APP 会把你的纪念日、文字和照片生成一个文件，保存到你选择的位置，比如「文件」APP 或 iCloud 云盘。这个文件归你所有，由你和你选择的服务按其条款保管，我们不会收到任何副本。

购买

倒数日 Pro 通过 App Store 销售，由苹果处理付款，我们看不到你的付款信息。APP 只会向苹果确认你是否有有效的购买，用来解锁 Pro 功能。你可以在 Apple 账户设置里管理或取消订阅。

没有追踪

  • 没有账号，不需要登录
  • 没有统计分析或崩溃上报服务
  • 没有广告，不跨 APP 或网站追踪你
  • 没有收集数据的第三方代码

儿童

倒数日不收集任何人的个人数据，包括儿童。

删除数据

你可以在 APP 里删除任意纪念日。删除 APP 会清除它在你 iPhone 上保存的所有内容。保存在其他地方的备份文件不受影响，如果不再需要，请自行删除。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页面和顶部的日期。

联系我们

有关于隐私的问题，请发邮件到 luckytonyma@gmail.com。
