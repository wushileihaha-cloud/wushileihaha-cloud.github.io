Privacy Policy

Pocket Days · Last updated November 10, 2026

English简体中文

The short version: Pocket Days doesn't collect any personal data. What you add stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account, where only you can see it. We don't have servers, accounts, ads or analytics.

Where your days are kept

The names, dates, notes and photos you add are stored on your iPhone, in storage shared by the app and its widgets. They are never sent to us.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your days, notes and photos are also kept in your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices and come back if you reinstall the app. This uses Apple's CloudKit. The data is stored in your iCloud account, counts toward your iCloud storage, and is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. We, as the developer, cannot see or access it.

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Pocket Days in your iPhone's Settings. Your chosen look is synced with iCloud in the same private way.

Photos

When you add a background or memory photo, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. The app only receives the photos you pick and keeps its own copy. It can't see the rest of your photo library.

If you save a share image, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photo library. This permission only lets the app add images, not read them.

Contacts

If you choose to import birthdays, iOS asks for permission to read your contacts. The app reads only names and birthdays to show you a list, and saves only the birthdays you pick. Nothing else from your contacts is stored, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn this permission off at any time in Settings.

Calendar

If you choose to add plans from Calendar, iOS asks for permission to read your calendar. The app lists your coming events and saves only the ones you pick. Your calendar is never changed and nothing is sent to us. You can turn this permission off at any time in Settings.

Face ID and passcode lock

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded. It never receives your face data, fingerprint or passcode.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri how long until a day. Siri requests are processed by Apple under its own privacy policy. The app only answers with the count for the day you ask about.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us to deliver them.

Widgets, Live Activities and Apple Watch

Widgets, Lock Screen widgets, Live Activities and the Apple Watch app show the days you choose. Days are sent straight from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch. Keep in mind that anyone who can see your screens can see these too.

Backups

When you back up, the app creates a file with your days, notes and photos and saves it where you choose, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. That file is yours. Storing it is handled by you and the service you choose, under their own terms. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

Pocket Days Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles payment. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether you have an active purchase so it can unlock Pro features. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

No tracking

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No third-party code that collects data

Children

Pocket Days doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any day in the app, and it's removed from iCloud and your other devices too. Deleting the app removes what it stored on that device, but your iCloud copy stays so it can come back. To delete the iCloud copy as well, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Pocket Days. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected, so delete those yourself if you no longer want them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email luckytonyma@gmail.com.

隐私政策

口袋纪念日 · 更新于 2026 年 11 月 10 日

简单来说：口袋纪念日不收集任何个人数据。你添加的内容只保存在你自己的设备和你自己的 iCloud 账户里，只有你能看到。我们没有服务器，没有账号，没有广告，也没有统计分析。

你的纪念日保存在哪里

你添加的名称、日期、文字和照片保存在你的 iPhone 上，由 APP 和它的小组件共用。这些内容不会发送给我们。

iCloud 同步

如果你登录了 iCloud，你的纪念日、文字和照片还会保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库里，这样能同步到你的其他设备，重装 APP 后也能恢复。这项功能使用苹果的 CloudKit，数据存放在你的 iCloud 账户里，占用你的 iCloud 储存空间，由苹果按照它的隐私政策处理。我们作为开发者看不到，也无法访问这些数据。

如果不想同步，可以在 iPhone 的「设置」里关闭口袋纪念日的 iCloud。你选的外观也会用同样私密的方式通过 iCloud 同步。

照片

添加背景照片或回忆照片时，你通过苹果的照片选择器来选。APP 只会拿到你选中的照片，并保存一份副本，看不到你相册里的其他照片。

保存分享图片时，iOS 会请求「添加到相册」的权限。这个权限只能往相册里添加图片，不能读取相册。

通讯录

如果你选择从通讯录导入生日，iOS 会请求读取通讯录的权限。APP 只读取姓名和生日用来列出名单，并且只保存你勾选的生日。通讯录里的其他信息不会保存，也不会发送给我们。你可以随时在「设置」里关闭这项权限。

日历

如果你选择从日历添加安排，iOS 会请求读取日历的权限。APP 会列出你接下来的日程，只保存你勾选的那些。APP 不会修改你的日历，也不会把任何内容发送给我们。你可以随时在「设置」里关闭这项权限。

面容 ID 和密码锁

如果你打开了 APP 锁，iOS 会验证你的面容 ID、触控 ID 或锁屏密码。APP 只知道验证是否通过，永远拿不到你的面部数据、指纹或密码。

Siri 和快捷指令

你可以问 Siri 离某个日子还有多久。Siri 的请求由苹果按照它的隐私政策处理，APP 只会回答你问的那个日子的天数。

提醒

提醒是在你的 iPhone 上安排的本地通知，送达时不会把任何内容发送给我们。

小组件、实时活动和 Apple Watch

桌面小组件、锁屏小组件、实时活动和 Apple Watch APP 会显示你选择的纪念日。纪念日由你的 iPhone 直接传到与它配对的 Apple Watch。请注意，能看到你屏幕的人也能看到这些内容。

备份

备份时，APP 会把你的纪念日、文字和照片生成一个文件，保存到你选择的位置，比如「文件」APP 或 iCloud 云盘。这个文件归你所有，由你和你选择的服务按其条款保管，我们不会收到任何副本。

购买

口袋纪念日 Pro 通过 App Store 销售，由苹果处理付款，我们看不到你的付款信息。APP 只会向苹果确认你是否有有效的购买，用来解锁 Pro 功能。你可以在 Apple 账户设置里管理或取消订阅。

没有追踪

  • 没有账号，不需要登录
  • 没有统计分析或崩溃上报服务
  • 没有广告，不跨 APP 或网站追踪你
  • 没有收集数据的第三方代码

儿童

口袋纪念日不收集任何人的个人数据，包括儿童。

删除数据

你可以在 APP 里删除任意纪念日，它也会从 iCloud 和你的其他设备上删除。删除 APP 只会清除这台设备上的内容，iCloud 里的副本会保留，方便以后恢复。如果也想删除 iCloud 里的副本，请前往「设置」→ 你的名字 →「iCloud」→「管理账户储存空间」→「口袋纪念日」。保存在其他地方的备份文件不受影响，如果不再需要，请自行删除。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页面和顶部的日期。
